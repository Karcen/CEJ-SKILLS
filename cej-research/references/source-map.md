# 来源地图与阅读边界

本包核心方法来自下列 12 篇论文的选定部分。并非逐篇全文精读，也不是对下载语料的代表性抽样；2026 年选读较多，不能据此推断年份趋势。技能中的流程、检查表和交易研究示例是本包的综合迁移，不是作者原话。

## S1 · Marc Morgan (2026)

Keynes’s vision of inequality (and why it matters). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beag011)

实际阅读：引言；3.2–3.4、4；表1–3；结论。提炼用途：分配维度、约束状态与政策迁移。

## S2 · Nina Eichacker (2026)

Climate transition, risk, and industrial policy: Some post-Keynesian insights for policy makers. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beag026)

实际阅读：引言；2–4；结论。提炼用途：不确定性、私人投资动机、工具与执行。

## S3 · Danny Dorling (2026)

The economics of enough. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beag029)

实际阅读：引言；生活水平和实施相关选段；结论。提炼用途：充足性、目标与实施条件。

## S4 · Arne Heise (2026)

‘Groundhog day’ all over again: Minimum wage research under ‘thought style compulsion’. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beag022)

实际阅读：摘要；引言；2、5。提炼用途：理论、辅助假设与证据回应。

## S5 · Hélène de Largentaye (2026)

Keynesianism without Keynes: French post-World War II reconstruction (1945–1958). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beag030)

实际阅读：引言与结论。提炼用途：政策相似性、思想影响与历史顺序。

## S6 · Senhu Wang et al. (2022)

What matters more for employees’ mental health: job quality or job quantity?. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beab054)

实际阅读：摘要；引言；方法与结果选段。提炼用途：数量、质量、偏好匹配；调查关联边界。

## S7 · Arend Stemerding (2023)

A method for measuring rents. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/bead025)

实际阅读：摘要；引言；结论。提炼用途：指标实现、正常回报与概念边界。

## S8 · Michele Bee and Raphaël Fèvre (2024)

Gold Rush vs. War: Keynes on reviving animal spirits in times of crisis. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beae001)

实际阅读：摘要；引言；结论选段。提炼用途：文本语境、历史机制与外推。

## S9 · Vanessa Gash et al. (2025)

Decomposing the barriers to equal pay: examining differential predictors of the gender pay gap by socio-economic group. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beaf025)

实际阅读：摘要与引言。提炼用途：均值与组别异质性；未提炼具体分解估计量。

## S10 · Niall Reddy and Joel Rabinovich (2026)

Challenging the short-termist thesis in financialization studies: Evidence from US non-financial corporations, 1998–2018. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beaf052)

实际阅读：摘要；引言；结论。提炼用途：治理机制、行动者和结果层次。

## S11 · Gonzalo Durán (2026)

Trade union’s power and income distribution: Evidence from Chile. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beag001)

实际阅读：摘要；引言；结论选段。提炼用途：谈判制度、分配指标、PSM边界。

## S12 · Hamid Raza and Jawad Khawaja (2026)

Beyond neutrality: Uncovering the sectoral and distributional consequences of monetary policy in Nordic countries. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cje/beag027)

实际阅读：摘要；引言；方法开头；结论。提炼用途：部门关系、制度与冲击识别；未提炼完整估计程序。
